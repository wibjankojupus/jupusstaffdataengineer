About us

The AI secretariat for law firms

JUPUS is an AI built specifically for lawyers, supporting them from client intake through legal research and into the drafting of briefs, all in one platform: from the first phone call to the finished case file.

We are trained on over one million German court rulings and the actual workflows of German law firms, so our output is precise and always backed by sources. We are GDPR-compliant and professionally compliant (BRAO and §203 StGB), hosted on EU servers.

We answer the phone, capture inquiries, and qualify new clients automatically
We structure information and documents and create the case file with no manual typing
We research case law and draft briefs from your own templates, ready for review in minutes

Your focus: all three

There is no single module for this role. Every one of these products emits the usage data that lands in the warehouse, and the metrics you would own are built from all of it. You would trace an event from the moment it fires in the product to the figure on a board slide.

The role

Own the numbers a company runs on

Our data platform runs on ClickHouse with dbt on top, Python extract-load pipelines feeding an S3 data lake, and orchestration in GitHub Actions. It works, and it has grown quickly. That means this is modernisation and consolidation work, not a greenfield build and not a maintenance seat.

Our customer health score is a multi-component weighted metric with feature gating, dynamic reweighting and seat scaling, and it reaches the board. You would own its meaning as well as its SQL: definition, denominator, eligibility rules, known limitations, written down once and authoritatively, with the open questions named rather than smoothed over.

You would report to our Head of Engineering and work alongside a colleague who owns the business-facing side of data, plus our infrastructure and application engineers. This is a senior individual contributor role with no line management in either direction.

Own the data platform end to end

Ingestion and the raw layer, the ClickHouse warehouse, and the dbt project on top of it. You are the person who can say whether a number is wrong or the pipeline is.

Make our numbers trustworthy

Reliability, correctness, and a history that holds up, so that a trend is a record of what actually happened rather than a re-derivation of it.

Own the metrics the company runs on

Starting with our customer health score: the definition, the denominator, the eligibility rules and the known limitations, not just the SQL underneath them.

Set the standard for how data work is done

One authoritative written definition per leadership-visible number, and the conventions whoever joins the data function next will inherit.

Your first big challenge

Make the past stop changing

Our pipeline recomputes all history every night from today's state. That means every trend chart we draw is a re-derivation of the past rather than a record of it, and a six-month trend quietly rewrites itself while nobody is watching. Leadership asks for those trends. They deserve an answer that holds still.

The fix is an immutable point-in-time snapshot layer, one row per firm per day, written once and never recomputed, plus the incremental models to make it affordable. Then the harder half. Our customer health score has ten weighted components, and its definition has never been written down in one authoritative place. Somebody has to decide what it means, defend that decision to people who rely on the number, and then make the number reproducible.

The problem is real, visible and unowned. You cannot fix it quietly: changing what a number means is a conversation with the business, not just a pull request.

What you are walking into

A working ClickHouse and dbt platform that has grown fast under real load. Two pieces of work are already queued: moving the customer-health models to incremental mode, and building the point-in-time snapshot layer. Beyond that, the raw layer, the warehouse architecture and the metric definitions are all unowned, and the first month is yours to spend finding out what is actually fragile.

You would be inheriting something that works and has never had a single person responsible for whether it is right.

Our core principles

Amp It Up

We’re never satisfied. Everyone at JUPUS has a relentless drive to reach higher, improve continuously, and expand their capabilities. Our commitment to excellence pushes us to reshape the legal industry by setting new benchmarks.

02

03

Mistakes are part of progress. Rather than avoiding them, we embrace errors as opportunities to learn and propel us even faster towards success. This mindset empowers us to innovate fearlessly and achieve unprecedented results.

05

We are changing a centuries-old industry forever. Therefore, we relentlessly question and dismantle established norms, to create unprecedented breakthroughs and transformative change.

Build where it is not settled
